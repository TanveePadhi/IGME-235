Escape Goblinland

Escape Goblinland is a pac-man themed game where you need to collect crystals and defeat goblins to get out. Now is there a way to get out? Yes there is. Get 100 crystal points.

Instructions

Points

Collect crystals the light blue ones are worth 1 point. The yellow ones allows you to attack the goblins they are worth 5 point. You Need 100 Points to Get Out.

Movement

You can use the arrow keys or WASD to move around. You speed does increase if you move diagonal (I did not in fact normalize that vector haha). You go off off scree and appear on the oppositte side of the screen. For example if you go off the left side you will appear again on the right AND if you go off the top side you will appear at the bottom. Same applies if you go diagonal.
